From persona to conversation

We start from a real chat log. A matched persona re-voices each user turn, and the request and its constraints stay the same. The assistant turns are then regenerated, and a judge screens the whole conversation. Pick an example from the paper and run it through the line.

Numbered marks link a span of the rewrite to the profile field it comes from. Hover either side.
R1 · keep the taskConservative matching

Language and script are detected first. The persona must be a native speaker of the prompt's language. Among those, the match comes from location, then top-1 embedding similarity, then the language pool. If nothing matches, the turn is kept verbatim.

R2 · diversify registerConstrained rewrite

Quotes, code, URLs and numbers are masked and restored. Quirks fire with p = 0.25 per turn so that personas do not turn into caricatures. Rewrites shorter than 50% of the original are rejected.

R3 · stay on trajectoryContinuity supervision

The real next user message serves as weak supervision. A judge checks that the regenerated reply still leads to it, and the reply is adapted for up to two rounds.

R4 · no hidden shortcutSix-class sanity gate

Language mismatch, format violation, truncation, fabricated experience, hallucination, unaddressed request. Only the conversation turns are stored. The persona is not.
