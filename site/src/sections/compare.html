PersonaFactory as stylistic extension of existing persona datasets

PersonaHub describes a person in one or two sentences. SynthPAI adds a paragraph on writing style. PersonaFactory keeps a separate style layer with per-axis typing keys. This station compares the formats, then converts Nemotron-Personas and PersonaHub records into PersonaFactory backgrounds and adds a style layer. A further step restyles conversation data that has no user persona.

Six steps, from the existing datasets to a version with a style layer.
